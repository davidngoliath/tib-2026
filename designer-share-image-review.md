# Share Image Review

Hi — this doc is a quick review sheet for the current social share images across the site.

## What to review

For each page, please let us know:

- Does the image feel right for the page story?
- Does it look strong as a social share thumbnail?
- Is the focal point clear at small sizes?
- Is the crop likely to work well on platforms like iMessage, Slack, Facebook, and X?
- Do any pages need a more custom image treatment?
